Do you handle both residential and commercial plumbing?

Yes! Fix & Flow Plumbing Co. is equipped to handle plumbing repairs, installations, and maintenance for both residential homes and commercial businesses.

What should I do in a plumbing emergency?

If you have a major leak or burst pipe, locate your main water shut-off valve and turn it off immediately to prevent water damage. Then, contact us right away at (404) 800-3569.

How do I know if my water heater needs replacing?

Signs your water heater may need replacement include inconsistent water temperatures, rusty water, strange rumbling noises, leaks around the base, or if the unit is over 10-15 years old.
