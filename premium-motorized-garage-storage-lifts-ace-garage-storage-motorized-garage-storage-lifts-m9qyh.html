Frequently Asked Questions

What are motorized garage storage lifts?

Motorized garage storage lifts are innovative overhead shelving systems equipped with electric motors. Instead of climbing ladders carrying heavy boxes, you can lower the storage platform to the ground, load your items, and use a switch or remote to hoist the platform safely to the ceiling, instantly freeing up floor space.

How much weight can these lifts hold?

Weight capacities vary by model and brand. Premium systems like Auxx Lift and Ceiling Sam are engineered for heavy-duty use, typically supporting anywhere from 400 lbs to over 700 lbs. Always check the specific product specifications on our website to ensure it meets your storage needs.

Are overhead lifts difficult to install?

While installation requires basic DIY skills and tools to locate ceiling joists and secure the mounting brackets properly, our products come with comprehensive instructions. For those uncomfortable with overhead installations, we recommend hiring a local handyman or professional installer to ensure maximum safety.

What brands do you carry?

We pride ourselves on offering only the highest quality products. For overhead storage, we feature industry leaders like Auxx Lift and Ceiling Sam. For outdoor storage solutions, we carry highly durable Duramax vinyl and insulated sheds.

Do you offer outdoor storage solutions?

Yes! In addition to our motorized garage storage lifts, we offer a wide selection of outdoor sheds. Whether you need a simple vinyl shed for garden tools or a fully insulated building for sensitive equipment, we have solutions to transform your backyard.
