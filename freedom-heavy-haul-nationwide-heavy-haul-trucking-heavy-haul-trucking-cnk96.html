Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do you serve?

We provide tailored logistics and transportation solutions primarily for the construction, mining, agriculture, and industrial sectors. However, we are equipped to handle large-scale freight for virtually any industry requiring specialized transport.

Where do you operate?

Freedom Heavy Haul operates nationwide. We have the capability, network, and expertise to transport your heavy equipment and oversize loads safely across the entire United States.

How much does heavy haul trucking cost?

Costs vary significantly based on the dimensions, weight, distance, required permits, and escort vehicle requirements of your specific load. We provide transparent, competitive pricing and encourage you to contact us directly for an accurate, custom quote.

Do you handle permits and routing?

Yes, absolutely. Transporting oversize and overweight loads requires meticulous planning. Our logistics team handles all necessary state and local permits, route surveys, and pilot car arrangements to ensure a seamless delivery.

Is my freight insured during transit?

Yes. Safety and security are our top priorities. Your freight is fully insured while in our care, providing you with complete peace of mind from pickup to final delivery.
