Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered heavy haul transport?

Heavy haul transport refers to the movement of freight that exceeds standard legal size or weight limits set by local and federal highway authorities. This typically includes massive items like industrial boilers, large boats, wind turbine components, and heavy construction machinery that require specialized trailers, permits, and often escort vehicles.

Do you operate nationwide?

Yes, Freedom Heavy Haul provides comprehensive transportation services across the entire United States. Our extensive network allows us to move your equipment seamlessly from coast to coast, handling all state-specific regulations along the route.

How do you handle permits and routing?

Our dedicated logistics team manages the entire permitting process. We conduct thorough route surveys to ensure bridges, overpasses, and road widths can accommodate your specific load. We secure all necessary state and local permits, arrange for civilian or police escorts, and coordinate with utility companies if wire lifting is required.

What types of equipment do you transport?

We are equipped to haul a vast array of oversized items, including but not limited to: excavators, cranes, bulldozers, manufacturing presses, large storage tanks, transformers, prefabricated structures, and oversized marine vessels.

How is pricing determined for oversized loads?

Pricing is highly customized based on several factors: the exact dimensions and weight of the cargo, the total distance of the transport, required permits for each state crossed, the need for escort vehicles (pilot cars), and any special equipment (like multi-axle trailers) required to move the load safely.
