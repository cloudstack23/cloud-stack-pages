Frequently Asked Questions

How much do auto transport services cost?

The cost of vehicle shipping depends on several factors, including the distance, vehicle size and weight, the type of transport (open vs. enclosed), and current market conditions. For the most accurate pricing, we recommend requesting a free, no-obligation quote through our website.

How long does it take to ship a car?

Transit times vary based on the distance. Generally, a coast-to-coast shipment takes about 7 to 10 days, while shorter regional transports may take 1 to 3 days. We provide estimated delivery windows when you book your transport.

What is the difference between open and enclosed transport?

Open transport is the industry standard where vehicles are shipped on an open-air trailer. It is safe and cost-effective. Enclosed transport places your vehicle inside a covered trailer, providing complete protection from weather, dust, and road debris, making it ideal for high-value or classic cars.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. By law, all auto transport carriers must carry liability and cargo insurance. Your vehicle is covered from the moment it is loaded onto the truck until it is safely delivered to your destination.

Do you offer door-to-door delivery?

Yes, we specialize in door-to-door service. The driver will pick up and deliver your vehicle as close to your specified addresses as legally and safely possible, considering the size of the commercial transport truck.
