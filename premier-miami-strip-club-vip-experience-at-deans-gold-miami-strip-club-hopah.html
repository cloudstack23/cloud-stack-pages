We enforce an upscale, smart-casual dress code to maintain our luxury atmosphere. No athletic wear, overly baggy clothing, flip-flops, or offensive graphics are permitted. Dress to impress for the best experience.

Do you offer VIP bottle service reservations?

Yes, we highly recommend booking VIP bottle service in advance, especially on weekends. Reservations guarantee your entry and secure premium seating. Contact us via phone or email to view our bottle menu and make a reservation.
