Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping paper rolls used for?
Our high-quality shipping paper rolls (often Kraft paper) are incredibly versatile. They are primarily used as an eco-friendly void fill to prevent items from shifting inside boxes, wrapping fragile items to protect against scratches, and interleaving between products.
Do you really offer same-day shipping?
Yes! If you place your order before 4:00 PM EST, our warehouse team will process and ship your packaging supplies on the exact same day.
Where does The Boxery ship from?
While we proudly serve our local community in Lyndhurst, NJ, we operate out of multiple warehouses across the United States. This strategic network allows us to deliver your supplies quickly and efficiently, no matter where you are located.
Is shipping free on all items?
We offer absolutely free shipping on all of our poly and bubble mailers. For other bulky items like corrugated boxes and large rolls of Kraft paper, shipping costs are calculated based on your location and order size to give you the most competitive bulk pricing possible.
Do you sell to individuals or just businesses?
We supply everyone! Whether you are an e-commerce giant needing pallets of supplies, a small retail shop, or a household preparing for a big move, we have the moving and janitorial supplies you need at bulk-discounted prices.
