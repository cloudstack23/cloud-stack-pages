Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to ship a car?

The cost varies based on several factors including the distance, vehicle size and weight, transport type (open vs. enclosed), and current market conditions. Contact us for a free, personalized quote.

How long does auto transport take?

Transit times depend on the distance. Generally, coast-to-coast shipping takes 7-10 days, while shorter distances may take 1-3 days. We provide estimated delivery windows when you book.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. As a reputable auto transport company, we ensure that all carriers in our network carry active cargo insurance to protect your vehicle from pickup to delivery.

Can I put personal items in my car?

Generally, it is recommended to remove all personal belongings. Carriers are not licensed to transport household goods, and personal items are not covered by the carrier's cargo insurance.

How do I prepare my car for shipping?

Wash your car to easily spot pre-existing damage, remove custom accessories and toll tags, ensure the gas tank is about 1/4 full, and check for any fluid leaks.
