Frequently Asked Questions

Many people ask our Edmonton painters about our process. Here are some common answers.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes, we provide comprehensive, no-obligation free estimates for all residential and commercial painting projects in Edmonton. We will assess your space, discuss your needs, and provide a detailed quote.

What kind of paint do you use?

We use premium, high-quality paints from trusted brands to ensure durability, excellent coverage, and a beautiful finish. If you have specific brand preferences or require low-VOC/eco-friendly paints, we are happy to accommodate.

Do you handle furniture moving and prep work?

Absolutely. Proper preparation is key to a great paint job. We handle moving heavy furniture to the center of the room, covering everything with drop cloths, taping edges, filling holes, and sanding surfaces before any paint is applied.

How long does a typical painting project take?

The timeline depends on the size and scope of the project. A standard room might take a day, while a full exterior or whole-house interior could take several days to a week. We will provide a clear timeline during your estimate.

Are your painters licensed and insured?

Yes, Mill Creek Painters Edmonton is fully licensed and insured. We prioritize safety and professionalism, giving you peace of mind while we work on your property.
