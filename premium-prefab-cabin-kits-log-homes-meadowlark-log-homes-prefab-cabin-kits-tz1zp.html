Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in your prefab cabin kits?

Our kits typically include the pre-cut and numbered wall logs, log siding for gables, heavy timber roof systems, porch materials, and necessary hardware. We provide detailed blueprints to make assembly as straightforward as possible.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes, Meadowlark Log Homes proudly manufactures in Montana and delivers our log home packages to job sites across the entire United States.

Can I customize my log home design?

Absolutely. While we offer standard plans, we specialize in custom designs. Our team will work closely with you to modify existing plans or draft a completely unique home from scratch.

What makes Amish log homes different?

Amish craftsmanship is renowned for its meticulous attention to detail, traditional joinery techniques, and a deep respect for the wood itself. This results in a tighter, stronger, and more aesthetically pleasing structure.

How long does it take to assemble your prefab cabin kits?

Assembly time for our prefab cabin kits varies based on the size of the home and the experience of the builder. However, because our logs are precision-milled and pre-cut, the dry-in process is significantly faster than traditional stick-framing.
