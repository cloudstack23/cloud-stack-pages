Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a heavy haul?

A heavy haul typically refers to freight that exceeds standard legal weight and dimension limits set by state and federal guidelines. This usually means loads wider than 8.5 feet, taller than 13.6 feet, or heavier than 80,000 pounds gross weight. These shipments require specialized trailers and permits.

Do you provide services nationwide?

Yes, Freedom Heavy Haul operates across the entire United States. We have a vast network of logistics experts and carriers capable of moving your equipment from coast to coast safely and efficiently.

How do you handle permits and routing?

Our dedicated logistics team handles 100% of the permitting and routing process. Because regulations vary by state and municipality, we conduct thorough route surveys, secure all necessary oversize/overweight permits, and arrange for civilian or police escorts when mandated by law.

What types of equipment do you transport?

We transport a wide variety of items including construction machinery (excavators, loaders), agricultural equipment, industrial generators, transformers, large boats, storage tanks, aerospace components, and manufacturing presses.

How is pricing determined for these shipments?

The cost of heavy haul transport depends on several factors: the exact dimensions and weight of the load, the distance traveled, required permits, escort vehicles, and the specific type of specialized trailer needed. Contact us for a customized, accurate quote.
