Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our transportation services.

What is considered a heavy haul?

A heavy haul generally refers to freight that exceeds standard legal dimensions (width, height, or length) or weight limits set by state and federal regulations. This typically requires specialized trailers, permits, and sometimes pilot cars.

Do you operate nationwide?

Yes, Freedom Heavy Haul provides transportation services across the entire United States. We have the capability and network to move your equipment from coast to coast.

How do you handle permits for oversized loads?

Our experienced logistics team handles the entire permitting process. We work directly with state and local DOT offices to secure all necessary oversize and overweight permits required for your specific route.

What types of equipment do you transport?

We transport a wide variety of equipment including construction machinery (excavators, dozers, cranes), agricultural equipment, industrial generators, transformers, and large manufacturing components.

How can I get a quote for my shipment?

Getting a quote is easy! Simply call us at (866) 305-6018 with the dimensions (length, width, height), weight, and the pickup/delivery locations of your cargo, and our team will provide a comprehensive estimate.
