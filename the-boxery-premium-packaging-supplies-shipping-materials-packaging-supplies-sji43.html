Frequently Asked Questions

How fast will my order ship?

We pride ourselves on speed. Any order placed before 4pm EST will ship the exact same day. We utilize multiple warehouses positioned strategically across the U.S. to ensure faster delivery times nationwide.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! We offer free shipping specifically on all of our mailer products, including poly mailers and bubble mailers, helping you keep your shipping costs down.

What types of packaging materials do you carry?

We are a comprehensive supplier. Our inventory includes over 1,000 sizes of corrugated boxes, tape, stretch wrap, void fill, and a wide variety of other packaging supplies for both commercial shipping and moving needs.

Do you sell products other than shipping boxes?

Absolutely. In addition to shipping materials, our team carries a robust selection of janitorial and office supplies. This includes commercial cleaning products, paper goods, and ice melt for facility maintenance.

Where is The Boxery located?

While we proudly serve our local community in Lyndhurst, NJ, we are a nationwide e-commerce supplier. We ship to customers all across the United States from our network of distribution centers.
