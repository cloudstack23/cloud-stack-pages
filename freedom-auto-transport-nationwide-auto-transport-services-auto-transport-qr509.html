Frequently Asked Questions

How much does auto transport cost?

The cost of shipping a vehicle depends on several factors, including the distance, vehicle size and weight, transport type (open or enclosed), and the time of year. For the most accurate pricing, we recommend requesting a free, no-obligation quote through our website.

How long does it take to ship a car?

Transit times vary based on the distance. Generally, a coast-to-coast transport takes about 7 to 10 days, while shorter distances may take 1 to 5 days. Weather, traffic, and road conditions can also impact delivery schedules.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes, absolutely. All carriers in our network are required to carry active cargo insurance. Your vehicle is covered from the moment it is loaded onto the truck until it is safely delivered and inspected at the destination.

Do you offer door-to-door delivery?

Yes, we specialize in convenient door-to-door delivery. The driver will pick up and drop off your vehicle as close to your specified addresses as legally and safely possible, depending on local street restrictions for large commercial trucks.

Can I put personal items in my car during shipping?

Generally, we advise against packing personal items in the vehicle. Transport trucks are subject to strict weight limits, and cargo insurance typically does not cover personal belongings left inside the car. Please clear your vehicle of valuables before pickup.
