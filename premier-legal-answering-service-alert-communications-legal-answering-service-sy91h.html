Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a legal answering service different from a standard one?
A specialized service like Alert Communications employs receptionists who are specifically trained in legal terminology, empathy for distressed callers, and the strict confidentiality requirements of the legal profession. We understand the high stakes of legal inquiries.
Do you offer bilingual answering services?
Yes. We provide highly trained bilingual (English and Spanish) intake specialists to ensure you can serve a broader demographic and never miss out on a potential case due to a language barrier.
Can you integrate with our firm's CRM or case management software?
Absolutely. We integrate with most major legal CRM and case management systems. This ensures that new intake information and messages are delivered directly into your existing workflow in real-time.
How do you handle urgent calls after hours?
We follow your exact protocols. If a call meets your criteria for an emergency, we can patch the call directly to the on-call attorney, send an urgent text, or dispatch an email, ensuring immediate action can be taken.
Are your intake scripts customizable?
Yes, our intake processes are fully customizable. We work closely with your firm to develop specific questionnaires tailored to your practice areas (e.g., personal injury, criminal defense, family law) to properly qualify leads.
