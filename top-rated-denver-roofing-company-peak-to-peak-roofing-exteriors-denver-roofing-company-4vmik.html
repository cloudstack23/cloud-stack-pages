Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about your roof.

How do I know if I need a roof replacement or just repairs?

Signs you may need a replacement include curling or missing shingles, excessive granule loss in gutters, sagging areas, or a roof that is over 20 years old. As an experienced Denver roofing company, we provide honest, thorough inspections to determine the most cost-effective solution for your property.

Do you help with hail damage insurance claims?

Yes, absolutely. Colorado weather can be unpredictable. Our team is highly experienced in identifying storm and hail damage. We will document the damage, meet with your insurance adjuster, and help guide you through the entire claims process to ensure your home is fully restored.

What types of roofing materials do you install?

We install a wide variety of premium materials suited for the Denver climate, including architectural asphalt shingles, durable metal roofing, tile, and specialized flat roofing systems (TPO, EPDM) for commercial properties.

How long does a typical roof installation take?

Most standard residential roof replacements can be completed in 1 to 2 days, depending on the size of the roof and weather conditions. We pride ourselves on efficient, clean work that minimizes disruption to your daily life.

Are you licensed and insured in Denver?

Yes. Peak To Peak Roofing & Exteriors is fully licensed, bonded, and insured to operate in Denver and the surrounding metro areas. We prioritize safety and compliance on every job site.
