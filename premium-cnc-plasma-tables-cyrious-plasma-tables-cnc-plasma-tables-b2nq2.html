Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your CNC plasma tables different?

Our CNC plasma tables are built with a focus on uncompromised rigidity and precision. We use heavy-duty materials and state-of-the-art motion control systems to ensure that every cut is accurate, reducing the need for secondary finishing operations.

Are these tables suitable for hobbyists?

Absolutely. While our tables are built to industrial standards, we have designed the software and user interface to be intuitive and accessible. Hobbyists and small shop owners can easily learn to operate our machines and produce professional-grade work.

What types of metal can I cut?

Depending on the plasma cutter you pair with our tables, you can cut a wide variety of conductive metals, including mild steel, stainless steel, aluminum, copper, and brass. Thickness capabilities will vary based on your specific plasma power source.

Do you provide support and training?

Yes, we pride ourselves on exceptional customer service. We provide comprehensive resources, setup guides, and support to ensure you are up and running quickly and safely.

Where are your tables manufactured?

Cyrious Plasma Tables are proudly designed and manufactured in the United States, ensuring strict quality control and reliable craftsmanship in every unit we produce.
