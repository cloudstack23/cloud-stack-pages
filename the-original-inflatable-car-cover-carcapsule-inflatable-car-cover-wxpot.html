Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is an inflatable car cover?

An inflatable car cover, often referred to as a "car bubble," is a clear, durable PVC enclosure that completely seals your vehicle inside. It uses a continuous duty fan to inflate the bubble and circulate filtered air, creating a pristine, climate-controlled environment.

How does the CarCapsule prevent rust and mold?

Our patented Evaporative Storage System (ESS) continuously exchanges the air inside the capsule. By keeping the air moving and the temperature inside consistent with the outside, it prevents condensation from forming on your vehicle, completely eliminating the conditions that cause rust and mildew.

Can the outdoor models withstand hail?

Yes! Our outdoor capsules are designed with heavy-duty materials and a cushion of air that acts as a shock absorber. They are highly effective at bouncing off hail, falling branches, and other debris that would normally cause severe dents and damage.

How much electricity does the fan use?

The continuous-duty fan is highly energy efficient. It runs on a 12-volt power supply and typically costs only a few dollars a month in electricity to operate, making it an incredibly cost-effective insurance policy for your classic car.

Is it difficult to set up and use?

Not at all. Setup takes about 15 minutes. You simply lay out the base mat, drive your vehicle onto it, zip the top cover to the base, and plug in the fan. The capsule inflates around your vehicle automatically.
