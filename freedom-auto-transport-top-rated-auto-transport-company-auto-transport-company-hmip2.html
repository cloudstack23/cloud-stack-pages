Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to ship a car?

The cost varies based on several factors including the distance, vehicle size, type of transport (open vs. enclosed), and current market conditions. Contact us for a precise, no-obligation quote tailored to your specific needs.

How long does auto transport take?

Transit times depend primarily on distance. A cross-country trip typically takes 7-10 days, while shorter distances may take 1-3 days. We always provide an estimated delivery window when you book with our auto transport company.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes, absolutely. All carriers in our network are required to maintain active cargo insurance to cover your vehicle against damage during transit, providing you with peace of mind.

How do I prepare my car for shipping?

We recommend cleaning your car inside and out, removing all personal items and custom accessories, ensuring the gas tank is only a quarter full, and documenting any existing damage with photos before pickup.

Do you offer door-to-door service?

Yes, we specialize in door-to-door service. The driver will get as close to your door as safely and legally possible, considering the size of the commercial transport truck.
